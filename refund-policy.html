Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund ONLY under the following conditions:

Missing Products

  • If any product listed in your order is missing from the delivered package, we will issue a full refund for that specific missing item.
  • You MUST provide an unboxing video as proof (see mandatory requirement below).

Product Out of Stock After Order

  • If a product becomes out of stock after you have placed and paid for your order, we will issue a full refund for that item.
  • Refund will be processed to your original payment method.

IMPORTANT: Apart from missing products and out-of-stock situations, NO other refund requests will be entertained. We do NOT offer refunds for change of mind, wrong ordering, or buyer's remorse.

Mandatory Unboxing Video — REQUIRED

This is the MOST IMPORTANT requirement for any refund claim:

You MUST record a complete, uncut unboxing video of your package from the moment you start opening it. Without this video, NO refund will be processed — no exceptions.

Unboxing Video Requirements

  1. Start recording BEFORE you open the package — show the sealed package clearly.
  2. Record the entire unboxing process in one continuous, uncut video.
  3. Show all items received clearly, one by one.
  4. Show the shipping label and invoice/receipt in the video.
  5. The video must be clear, well-lit, and unedited.
  6. If any product is missing, state it clearly in the video.

Send your unboxing video to our WhatsApp (+91 63817 33447) along with your Order ID when raising a refund claim.

When Refund Will NOT Be Given

Refund will be REJECTED in the following cases:

  • ❌ No unboxing video — If you don't have a complete unboxing video, refund is automatically denied.
  • ❌ Video has cuts or edits — If the unboxing video is edited, cut, or not continuous, refund will be denied.
  • ❌ Products used, soldered, or modified — Once you use, solder, or modify the product, no refund.
  • ❌ Physical damage by customer — Damage caused by mishandling, wrong wiring, or improper usage.
  • ❌ Change of mind — We don't refund because you changed your mind after ordering.
  • ❌ Wrong product ordered by customer — If you ordered the wrong item, no refund.
  • ❌ Package opened without video — If you opened the package without recording, no proof = no refund.
  • ❌ Shipping charges — Shipping fee (₹120) and COD fee (₹60) are non-refundable.
  • ❌ Stripe processing fee (2%) — Payment processing fee is non-refundable.
  • ❌ Refund request after 3 days — Claim must be raised within 3 days of delivery.

Our decision on refund claims is FINAL. We review the unboxing video and evidence carefully before making any decision.

How to Claim a Refund

Follow these steps if you have a valid refund claim:

Step 1: Record Unboxing Video

Record a complete, uncut unboxing video showing all items received (or missing items).

Step 2: Contact Us Within 3 Days

WhatsApp us at +91 63817 33447 within 3 days of delivery with your Order ID and unboxing video.

Step 3: We Review Your Claim

Our team will review your unboxing video and order details within 1-2 business days.

Step 4: Refund Processed

If approved, refund will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method (minus Stripe 2% fee and shipping charges).
